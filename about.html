Our Humble Beginnings

Where Nostalgia Meets Pristine Hygiene

We started Chaat Adda with one simple conviction: India's favourite street food deserves royal celebration and spotless cleanliness.

Growing up, our happiest memories were gathered around pushcart vendors at sunset, holding leaf donas and eagerly waiting for the next pani-filled puri. But we also saw friends hesitate due to tap-water worries and questionable hygiene.

At Chaat Adda, we combined the soul of ancient street-food recipes with certified RO-mineral purification, stainless-steel prep stations, and eco-friendly leaf serveware. Every tamarind is stone-pitted, every cumin seed slow-roasted in desi ghee, and every smile 100% genuine.

Uncompromising Standards

The Four Pillars of Chaat Adda

What makes our puris crunchier, our dahi silkier, and our flavours unforgettable.

Daily Spice Roasting

We slow-roast whole cumin, black peppercorns, dried mint, and rock salt fresh every morning on traditional iron griddles.

100% RO Mineral Water

Every drop of teekha pudina pani and meetha imli pani is prepared using pharmaceutical-grade multi-stage RO water.

Pure Artisanal Dahi

Thick, farm-fresh curd slow-churned with organic cane sugar and aromatic roasted jeera for a rich, velvet finish.

Eco-Friendly Donas

Zero single-use plastic. We serve in compostable sal-leaf donas and earthen terracotta kulhads that honor nature.

Culinary Craftsmanship

The Anatomy of a Perfect Chaat

True chaat is not just street food — it is an ancient Indian culinary science balancing five fundamental tastes in a single bite:

  • Crisp Crunch (Karara): Hand-rolled semolina and whole wheat puris puffed in groundnut oil.
  • Tangy Sweetness (Meetha): Slow-cooked desert dates and tamarind simmered with jaggery.
  • Spicy Punch (Teekha): Mountain green chilies, wild mint, and black pepper.
  • Cooling Velvet (Thanda): Sweetened, hand-whisked curd soothing the palate.
  • Savoury Earth (Chatpata): Black salt from the salt ranges and aromatic asafoetida.
