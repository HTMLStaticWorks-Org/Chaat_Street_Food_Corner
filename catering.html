How Our Live Food Counters Work

We bring the complete street-food ecosystem to your venue 90 minutes before guest arrival—from brass vessels and RO pani coolers to eco-friendly leaf tableware.

STATION 01 • INTERACTIVE MATKA BAR

5-Flavour Live Golgappa & Chilled Dahi Puri Counter

Guests gather around hammered brass matkas filled with five chilled waters: Spicy Pudina, Khatta-Meetha Imli, Hing Jeera, Raw Mango Kairi, and Lasooni Tadka. Each golgappa is stuffed and served one by one to match every guest's exact spice preference.
