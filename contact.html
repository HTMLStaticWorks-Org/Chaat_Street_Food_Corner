FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Visiting

Quick answers to our guests' most common questions about hygiene, takeaway packaging, and dietary preferences.

How do you keep Pani Puri & Sev Puri crispy during delivery?

We pack all crispy puris, papdis, and nylon sev in sealed air-tight dry pouches, while chutneys, chilled curd, and spiced waters travel in separate spill-proof bottles so you can assemble fresh at home.

Do you offer Pure Jain (No Onion, No Garlic, No Potato) chaat?

Yes! Over 14 items on our menu have a dedicated Jain preparation using raw banana mash, boiled moong sprouts, and zero-garlic green and sweet tamarind chutneys prepared with separate utensils.

What water is used to prepare your Golgappa Pani?

100% of our spiced waters (Pudina, Imli, Hing Jeera, and Jaljeera) as well as our ice cubes are made exclusively with multi-stage UV + RO purified mineral water tested daily.

How far in advance should we book a Live Catering Counter?

For house parties and office events (30–80 guests), 48 hours notice is sufficient. For wedding sangeets and large festivals (150+ guests), we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks ahead.
