4-Layer Flavour Symphony

The Art of Making Chaat

Every great chaat is a delicate race against time — layering hot and cold, crisp and creamy, fiery and sweet seconds before you take your first bite.

Step 01

The Crisp Crack & Stuffing

Each sun-golden suji or atta puri is cracked by thumb and filled with warm, cumin-spiced potato mash and tender white vatana peas.

Step 02

The Fresh Herb & Spice Toss

Finely chopped red onions, farm-fresh green coriander, raw kairi mango, and hand-roasted black salt chaat masala awaken the palate.

Step 03

The Chilled Brass Lota Pour

Poured high from a gleaming brass lota — a splash of fiery mint-coriander jaljeera balanced with velvety khajoor-imli nectar.

Step 04

The Leaf Dona Experience

Served immediately in biodegradable sal-leaf donas or crisp paper cones — finished with a compulsory "Sukha Puri" on the house!
