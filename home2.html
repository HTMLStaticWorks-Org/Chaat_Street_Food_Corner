Step Inside the Warmth of an Indian Evening Bazaar

In India, an "Adda" is more than a food stall—it is where friends gather after college, families celebrate weekend evenings, and strangers bond over the shared thrill of spicy pani puri.

THE NIGHT BAZAAR ATMOSPHERE

Glowing Marquee Lights, Brass Matkas & Evening Hustle

Step up to our open-front counter and watch the theatre unfold: brass pots brimming with ice-cold pudina pani, mounds of golden nylon sev, and hand-painted bazaar signage that transports you straight to Old Delhi's Chandni Chowk and Mumbai's Juhu Chowpatty.

THE SIZZLING IRON TAWA STATION

Slow-Seared Aloo Tikki, Ragda & Golden Samosa Chaat

Around our 36-inch heavy cast-iron tawa, herb-crusted potato tikkis sear in pure ghee until they develop a shatteringly crisp golden crust. We crush them hot off the griddle and ladle over simmering white-pea ragda, ginger juliennes, and pomegranate jewels.
