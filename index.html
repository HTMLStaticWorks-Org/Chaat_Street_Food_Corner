About Chaat Adda

Welcome to the World of Chaat Adda

Born from a passion for India's vibrant bazaar corners, Chaat Adda brings together the nostalgic crunch, tangy chutneys, and warm hospitality of traditional street chaat inside a modern, hygienic dining experience.

Heritage & Craft

Our Story

From the bustling lanes of Mumbai Chowpatty to the royal chaat gullies of Banaras and Old Delhi, our founders spent years curating regional recipes so every plate at Chaat Adda tells an unforgettable story.
